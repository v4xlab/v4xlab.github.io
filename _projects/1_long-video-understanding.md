Making sense of video isn't just about a single clip anymore &mdash; it's about hours of footage, live streams, and content that keeps unfolding. This line of work tackles the unique challenges of long-form and streaming video: bypassing context-length limits, deciding what to answer and when, and building hierarchical, efficient representations that scale to real-world video lengths.
