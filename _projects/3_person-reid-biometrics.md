Recognizing who someone is &mdash; not just what they're doing &mdash; from video, even as clothing, appearance, and activity change. This theme covers clothes-changing person re-identification, disentangling identity from motion in activity-biometrics, and gait-based recognition, including its robustness to real-world noise and silhouette extraction errors.
