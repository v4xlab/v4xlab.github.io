The foundation of our group's research: teaching machines to recognize, localize, and understand human actions in video. This spans semi- and weakly-supervised detection, active learning under limited labels, robustness to occlusion, motion transferability, novel-view action synthesis, and efficient zero-shot recognition &mdash; the building blocks for every higher-level video understanding system we build.
