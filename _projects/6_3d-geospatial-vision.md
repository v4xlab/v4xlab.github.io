Reconstructing and understanding the 3D world from imagery captured at wildly different altitudes and viewpoints &mdash; from satellite and aerial views down to the ground. This emerging theme combines Gaussian splatting, diffusion-guided reconstruction, and multi-view geometry to build coherent 3D site models from sparse, mismatched observations.
