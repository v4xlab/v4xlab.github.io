Dr. Yogesh S. Rawat is an Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Science and the Center for Research in Computer Vision (CRCV) at the University of Central Florida. His research sits at the intersection of computer vision, deep learning, and multimodal AI, with a particular focus on video understanding, human activity recognition, biometrics, and building visual perception systems that are robust and label-efficient. He is especially interested in techniques that reduce the field's dependence on massive annotated datasets, spanning semi-supervised and weakly-supervised learning for video understanding.

He received his PhD in Computer Science from the School of Computing, National University of Singapore (2012&ndash;2017), advised by Mohan Kankanhalli, and completed his postdoctoral training at CRCV, UCF (2017&ndash;2019) under Mubarak Shah. Before that, he spent several years as a Senior Member of Technical Staff at Mentor Graphics. He earned his BTech in Computer Science and Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology (BHU), Varanasi, in 2009.

Dr. Rawat's research on activity-based biometrics introduced novel methods for identifying people from how they move, supported by IARPA's BRIAR program and NSF ART funding. He has led or co-led research funding from agencies including NSF, IARPA, DARPA, ARO, and Microsoft Research, spanning programs such as BRIAR, WRIVA, KAIROS, and NAIRR.

He serves as Associate Editor for Pattern Recognition, IEEE Transactions on Circuits and Systems for Video Technology, and Machine Vision and Applications, and has served as Area Chair for CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, NeurIPS, AAAI, WACV, ACM Multimedia, and BMVC. He was named an Outstanding Area Chair at CVPR 2026, one of the top three Associate Editors of IEEE-TCSVT in both 2023 and 2025, and was elevated to Senior Member, IEEE, in 2024. He is deeply committed to mentoring the next generation of researchers, with a particular passion for involving undergraduate and underrepresented students in research.
