## Current Course

### [CAP6412: Advanced Computer Vision](https://www.crcv.ucf.edu/courses/cap6412-spring-2026/) &mdash; Spring 2026

An advanced graduate-level course that puts students at the cutting edge of computer vision, multimodal learning, and embodied intelligence. Building on foundational deep learning, students explore how modern vision systems perceive, reason, generate, and act in complex environments &mdash; through transformers for vision, contrastive learning, large vision-language models, diffusion-based generative models, and world models that combine perception with decision-making.

**Part 1 &mdash; Foundations.** Transformer architectures (Vision Transformer and variants), contrastive and foundation models (CLIP and related pretraining frameworks), multimodal vision-language learning (BLIP-2, LLaVA), diffusion-based generative models, and modern 3D representations including neural scene representations and Gaussian splatting.

**Part 2 &mdash; Frontier research.** Each class centers on a paper presented by a student group, spanning large vision-language models, diffusion and generative models, world models for action and planning, safety and robustness (bias, privacy, security, toxicity, hallucination), and multimodal action-oriented models. Paper selections emphasize recent research with publicly available code, so students can reproduce, analyze, and extend the methods in hands-on assignments and projects.

By the end of the course, students will be able to:

- Understand vision-language and diffusion generative models, from foundational concepts to recent research advances
- Read and critically engage with current research papers in advanced computer vision and multimodal AI
- Identify the strengths and weaknesses of a scientific contribution &mdash; its assumptions, experimental design, and real-world impact
- Generate novel research ideas that extend or challenge existing work
- Collaborate on a team research implementation project, culminating in code, experiments, and a written report
- Write a comprehensive review and summary of a research paper

## Course History

### CAP6412: Advanced Computer Vision

An advanced graduate seminar on cutting-edge computer vision research, with the specific focus shifting each year to track the state of the art &mdash; from capsule networks and self-supervised learning to, most recently, vision-language and diffusion models.

- [Spring 2026](https://www.crcv.ucf.edu/courses/cap6412-spring-2026/)
- [Spring 2020](https://www.crcv.ucf.edu/courses/cap6412-spring-2020/)

### CAP5415: Computer Vision

A graduate-level introduction to computer vision, covering the core concepts &mdash; image formation, feature detection, segmentation, object recognition, and deep learning for vision &mdash; through lectures, homework, programming assignments, and a final project.
