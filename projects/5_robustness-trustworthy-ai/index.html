Robustness & Trustworthy AI

7 papers

Models that work in the lab often break in the wild. This theme is about making vision and vision-language systems robust to visual and language perturbations, adversarial attacks, distribution shift, and noisy real-world conditions — plus efficient test-time adaptation methods that let models recover performance without expensive retraining.
