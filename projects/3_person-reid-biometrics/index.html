Person Re-Identification & Biometrics

7 papers

Recognizing who someone is — not just what they’re doing — from video, even as clothing, appearance, and activity change. This theme covers clothes-changing person re-identification, disentangling identity from motion in activity-biometrics, and gait-based recognition, including its robustness to real-world noise and silhouette extraction errors.

(Pathak & Rawat, 2025) (Pathak & Rawat, 2025) (Liang & Rawat, 2025) (Azad & Rawat, 2025) (Azad & Rawat, 2024) (Mitra & Rawat, 2025) (Sayera et al., 2026)
