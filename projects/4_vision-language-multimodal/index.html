Vision-Language AI

12 papers

Bridging pixels and language. This work probes and extends vision-language models for spatio-temporal grounding, hallucination-aware reasoning, depth and spatial perception, safety in industrial settings, action-oriented planning, and generative sketch and vision-language-action models — pushing multimodal systems toward genuine understanding rather than pattern matching.
