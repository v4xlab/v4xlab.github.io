Early Work: Multimedia & Social Photography

10 papers

Before video understanding became the focus, this early-career work explored context-aware photography, crowdsourced and social-media-driven image capture assistance, and multimedia tag recommendation — research that shaped a lasting interest in how people interact with and are represented by visual media.
